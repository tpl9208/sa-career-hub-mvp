Editorial policy

SA Career Hub aims to make opportunity information useful, clear and safe for South African job seekers. Listings are informational and are not endorsements of an employer or a promise of employment.

Opportunity listings

  • Before publishing, editors should check the opportunity and its requirements against a credible employer or provider source.
  • Where available, the listing should include a direct link to the original announcement, separately from the application destination.
  • A “Listing checked” date should be recorded only after checking the information and should not be guessed or backdated.
  • Closing dates, eligibility rules and application links can change. Visitors should confirm them with the employer before applying.
  • Listings with a known closing date are removed from active lists after the closing date and a short grace period. This automated rule does not confirm whether an opportunity is still open.

Some current records do not yet show an original-announcement link or a checked date. Their absence means that no date is being claimed; it is not evidence that the listing was recently verified.

Corrections and safety

We welcome reports of inaccurate or expired information through the contact and corrections page. Always verify a vacancy on the employer's official website. Never pay someone to secure a job, learnership or interview.

Career guidance

Career resources are general educational information, not legal, financial or recruitment advice. Requirements vary by employer and programme; follow the official advert when it differs from general guidance on this site.

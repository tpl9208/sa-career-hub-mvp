About SA Career Hub

SA Career Hub helps people in South Africa discover learnerships, internships, graduate programmes, bursaries, training opportunities and entry-level jobs. The site also publishes practical guidance for preparing applications and avoiding recruitment scams.

Independent information

SA Career Hub is an independent information website. Unless a listing explicitly says otherwise, the site is not the employer, recruiter, training provider or application platform, and it is not affiliated with the organisations whose opportunities it lists.

Opportunity details can change or close after publication. Always check eligibility, dates and instructions on the employer's official website before sharing personal information or applying. SA Career Hub does not guarantee that a listing is current or that an application will be successful.

Corrections and feedback

If you spot an inaccurate listing or have a suggestion, use the contact and corrections page.
